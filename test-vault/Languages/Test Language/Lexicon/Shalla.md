# Shalla

## Meaning

Expresses closeness or proximity. The closeness may be **physical or relational**, depending on what is being described.

The precise grammatical classification of **shalla** has not yet been established.

## Usage

### Physical proximity

**Shalla Tap Ma** — “shallow water”

Literal meaning: **“close to the top mother.”**

In this construction, **shalla** expresses physical proximity.

### Relational closeness

**Shalla Freda** — “close friend”

In this construction, **shalla** expresses personal or relational closeness rather than physical distance.

## Notes

The attested uses suggest a broader underlying concept of **closeness/proximity** rather than separate words for physical nearness and personal closeness.

Further examples should be allowed to establish the grammatical behavior of **shalla** before assigning it a fixed part of speech.
